I actually think this is the right approach from both a sales and technical perspective.

Instead of selling **one huge system**, sell a **maturity journey**.

```
Tier 1 → Digital Presence
Tier 2 → Customer Engagement (CRM + AI)
Tier 3 → Pharmacy Operations Platform
```

This makes it much easier for a pharmacy to buy because they can start small and grow into the platform.

---

# Tier 1 — Pharmacy Digital Presence

## Purpose

Provide a professional online presence that makes it easy for customers to:

- Find the pharmacy
- Learn about its services
- Contact the pharmacy
- Build trust
- Perform simple, non-clinical actions

> **Tier 1 is intentionally informational. It does not manage the pharmacy's internal operations.**

---

# Objectives

The website should:

- Improve online visibility
- Increase customer trust
- Reduce repetitive enquiries
- Make the pharmacy easier to contact
- Provide accurate business information
- Encourage customers to visit or call

---

# Future-Ready Design

Although Tier 1 is a standalone website, its design should anticipate future upgrades to avoid major redesigns.

Reserve space in the navigation and architecture for features introduced in later tiers, such as:

- Customer Login
- AI Assistant
- My Refills
- Customer Portal
- Online Appointments

These links can remain hidden until the corresponding tier is activated.

---

# Out of Scope (Tier 1)

The following features belong to higher tiers and should **not** be included in Tier 1:

- Customer accounts
- CRM and customer profiles
- AI chatbot or virtual assistant
- Automated reminders (SMS, WhatsApp, Email)
- Loyalty and rewards programs
- Online payments
- Prescription workflow management
- Inventory management
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Staff management
- Reporting and analytics dashboards
- Supplier management
- Multi-branch operations
- Internal pharmacy workflows
- Business automation

---

This gives you a clean **Tier 1 product** that you can realistically deliver in **2–4 weeks**, while creating a solid foundation for **Tier 2 (Customer Engagement: CRM + AI)** and **Tier 3 (Internal Pharmacy Operations Platform)** without requiring customers to rebuild or replace their website later.
